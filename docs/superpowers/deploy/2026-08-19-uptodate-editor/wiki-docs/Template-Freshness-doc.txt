Renders the freshness box that reports how current a utaite's covered song list is, and files the page into the matching maintenance category. The box ages on its own: the status, colour and meter are all derived from the supplied date, so it degrades from '''Up to date''' through '''Slightly outdated''' to '''Outdated''' without anyone editing it.

'''Most editors should not use this template directly.''' Use {{T|Uptodate}} on a <code>/Songs</code> subpage, and {{T|Uptodate/sync}} on the article to mirror it. {{T|Uptodate}} is a thin wrapper that forwards its parameters here. This page documents the underlying interface.

All logic lives in [[Module:Freshness]] and [[Module:Freshness/core]]; styling comes from [[Template:Freshness/styles.css]].

==Syntax==
<pre>
{{Freshness|<date>|base=|discography=|needrom=|nocat=
           |force-uptodate=|force-outdated=|status=|reason=
           |customlatesteditor=|lastedittext-nolink=}}
</pre>

==How the status is chosen==
The age is measured in whole months between the supplied date and today.

{| class="dataTable sortable"
! Age !! Status shown !! Category
|-
| under 6 months || Up to date || up-to-date
|-
| 6 to under 18 months || Slightly outdated || outdated
|-
| 18 months and over || Outdated || outdated
|}

Two flags override that:
* <code>force-uptodate=yes</code> pins the list as complete. The header reads '''Marked as completed''', the meter stays full and the colour stops fading. Intended for singers who are no longer active, where the list is finished and will not grow.
* <code>force-outdated=yes</code> (or <code>status=outdated</code>, or any <code>reason=</code>) forces '''Outdated'''. An explicit outdated flag always beats a pin.

A missing or unparseable date is treated as outdated.

==Accepted date formats==
* <code>October 31, 2023</code> — month name full or abbreviated; the comma and an ordinal suffix such as <code>31st</code> are both optional
* <code>2023-10-31</code>, <code>2023/10/31</code>, <code>2023.10.31</code>
* <code>2023年10月31日</code>

==Colour==
<code>base</code> (or <code>bordercolor</code>) sets the box colour, which fades toward grey as the list ages and is fully grey at 30 months.

* A '''6-digit hex''' value such as <code>#A97A3F</code> is faded numerically.
* '''Any other valid CSS colour''' — an X11 name like <code>magenta</code>, a variable like <code>var(--primary-color)</code>, <code>rgb()</code>, <code>hsl()</code>, or short hex — is faded in the browser using <code>color-mix()</code>. This needs a recent browser; older ones fall back to the stylesheet default.
* Anything unrecognised is ignored and the default <code>#c3e6f9</code> is used instead.

==Examples==
<pre>
{{Freshness|October 31, 2023}}
{{Freshness|October 31, 2023|bordercolor=#A97A3F}}
{{Freshness|May 12, 2021|force-uptodate=yes}}
{{Freshness|May 12, 2021|reason=Missing the 2024 covers}}
{{Freshness|October 31, 2023|discography=yes|needrom=true}}
</pre>

==Parameters==
{| class="dataTable sortable"
! Parameter !! Aliases !! Label !! Description !! Type !! Default !! Status
|-
| <code>1</code> || – || Date || Date the song list was last checked. See the accepted formats above. Leaving it empty forces '''Outdated'''. || date || – || required
|-
| <code>base</code> || <code>bordercolor</code> || Colour || Box colour, faded by age. See the colour section above. || string || <code>#c3e6f9</code> ||
|-
| <code>force-uptodate</code> || – || Marked as completed || Set to <code>yes</code> to pin the list as complete so it stops ageing. Use for singers who are no longer active. || string || – ||
|-
| <code>force-outdated</code> || <code>status=outdated</code> || Force outdated || Set to <code>yes</code> to force the '''Outdated''' status regardless of the date. || string || – ||
|-
| <code>reason</code> || – || Reason || Replaces the description line with your own text, and forces '''Outdated'''. || string || – ||
|-
| <code>discography</code> || – || Discography || Any non-empty value widens the wording to cover the discography as well as the song list. || string || – ||
|-
| <code>needrom</code> || – || Needs romanization || Set to <code>true</code> to also file the page under [[:Category:Songlists requiring romanization]]. || string || – ||
|-
| <code>nocat</code> || – || No category || Set to <code>true</code> to render the box without categorizing. Use in documentation and examples. || string || – ||
|-
| <code>customlatesteditor</code> || – || Custom editor || Overrides the "last edited by" name in the footer. || string || <code><nowiki>{{REVISIONUSER}}</nowiki></code> ||
|-
| <code>lastedittext-nolink</code> || – || Plain edit text || Set to <code>true</code> to render "last edited" as plain text instead of a diff link. || string || – ||
|}

==Categories==
Unless <code>nocat=true</code> is set, the box files the page into one of the following through {{T|ForAllUtaite}}:
* [[:Category:Utaite with up-to-date covered song list]] / [[:Category:Youtaite with up-to-date covered song list]]
* [[:Category:Utaite with outdated covered song list]] / [[:Category:Youtaite with outdated covered song list]]

With <code>needrom=true</code> it also adds [[:Category:Songlists requiring romanization]].
